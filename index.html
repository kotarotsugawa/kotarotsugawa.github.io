津川 光太郎 (中央大学理工学部数学科教授)
Kotaro TSUGAWA (Professor at Department of Mathematics, Faculty of Science and Engineering, Chuo University)

  • Research Intersets（研究の興味）
    • nonlinear dispersive equations, nonlinear hyperbolic equations, Cauchy problem, well-posedness, harmonic analysis
    • Recently, I am interested in dispersive P.D.Es which appeare in the study of completely integrable systems, for instance, the KdV hierarchy, the cubic NLS hierarchy, the derivatibe NLS hierarchy and the Benjamin-Ono hierarchy.
